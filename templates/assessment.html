1. Resume / Skills Input

Paste your own resume text or a short skills summary. SkillGuide scans this user-provided text against its skill vocabulary and saves the detected skills to your profile.

How is the information extracted?
This prototype uses the resume/skills text entered by the student, normalizes it, and checks it against a predefined skill vocabulary using transparent text matching. The detected skills are then saved to that student's profile.
